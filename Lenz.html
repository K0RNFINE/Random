Faraday's Law
ε = −N · dΦ/dt
where Φ = B · A · cos(θ)
Lenz's Law
The induced EMF produces a current whose magnetic field opposes the change in flux.
How it works
1
Magnet moves → flux Φ through coil changes
2
Changing flux induces EMF: ε = −dΦ/dt
3
EMF drives current in coil (right-hand rule)
4
Current creates opposing B field (Lenz)
5
Net effect resists the original motion
Current Direction Rules
▼ Approach N-pole → CCW (opposing)
▲ Recede N-pole → CW (maintaining)
▼ Approach S-pole → CW (opposing)
▲ Recede S-pole → CCW (maintaining)
